# Visual Assets Guide for LinkedIn Post 📸

To make your LinkedIn post truly stand out, I recommend including a mix of high-quality screenshots and short screen recordings (GIFs or MP4s). Here is the ideal list of assets:

### 1. Hero Video: The "Tinder-Swipe" in Action (Crucial!)
*   **What to capture**: A screen recording of the expanded mobile player. Perform a few horizontal swipes on the album art to show the 3D card stack animation and how the next song "pops" into view.
*   **Why**: This is your most unique UI feature. It immediately grabs attention in a LinkedIn feed.

### 2. High-Fidelity Visualizer (Screenshot or Video)
*   **What to capture**: The "Particles" or "Circular" visualizer mode while a song with a strong beat is playing.
*   **Pro Tip**: Capture this in a dark room or with the OLED theme enabled to make the glowing "accent color" sync really pop.
*   **Mode Recommendation**: The **Particles** mode with a vibrant album cover (like something with pinks or oranges) looks world-class.

### 3. The Professional Equalizer (Screenshot)
*   **What to capture**: Open the Equalizer overlay. Set a "V-shape" or custom curve on the sliders.
*   **Why**: It communicates "Premium" and "Utility." It shows that the app isn't just a pretty face—it has deep technical features.

### 4. Authentication & Sync (Screenshot)
*   **What to capture**: A screenshot of the "Account" section within the Settings Drawer, showing the "Synced" status and your profile.
*   **Why**: It subtly proves the robustness of the backend (Supabase integration) and the maturity of the project.

### 5. Multi-Device "Action Shot" (Optional but Powerful)
*   **What to capture**: If possible, a photo of the app running on both a laptop (Desktop view) and a phone (Mobile view) side-by-side.
*   **Why**: Highlights the PWA nature and the responsiveness of the UI.

---
**Recommended Sequence for LinkedIn Gallery:**
1.  **Main Video**: Tinder Swipe + Visualizer (10-15 seconds).
2.  **Screenshot**: Particles Visualizer (Full screen).
3.  **Screenshot**: Pro Equalizer UI.
4.  **Screenshot**: Main Library view (showing the polished "Modern" layout).
