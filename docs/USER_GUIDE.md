# Vibe On User Guide 🎧

Welcome to **Vibe On**, a premium music experience designed for discovery and high-fidelity listening. This guide covers the key features and how to make the most of the app.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Installation
- **Web**: Access the app via your browser at the hosted URL.
- **Android**: Install the APK/AAB on your Android device (supports Android 10 and above).

### Authentication
Sign in with your **Google Account** to unlock Cloud Sync. This allows you to:
- Save your favorite songs.
- Create and manage playlists.
- Sync your equalizer settings and themes across all your devices.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. 3D Discovery (Tinder-style)
Navigate to the **Explore** page to find new music using a gesture-driven 3D card stack.
- **Swipe Right**: Play the song immediately.
- **Swipe Left**: Skip to the next recommendation.
- **Click Card**: View the full album or artist details.

### 2. Apple Music-style Synced Lyrics
While a song is playing, open the player and tap the **Lyrics** icon.
- Lyrics will scroll automatically in time with the music.
- Tap any lyric line to skip the song to that specific moment.

### 3. Pro 10-Band Equalizer
Fine-tune your audio to perfection.
- Access the Equalizer from the **Settings** or the **Player**.
- Choose from presets like Bass Boost, Acoustic, or Rock, or create your own custom profile.
- Changes are applied in real-time.

### 4. Group Sessions (Real-time Listening)
Listen to music together with friends, perfectly synced.
- **Create a Session**: Go to your Profile or Settings and click "Start Group Session". You will receive a unique Room Code.
- **Join a Session**: Enter a Room Code shared by a friend.
- **Sync**: The Host controls playback (Play/Pause/Seek/Next), and all Guests' players will follow instantly.
- **Reactions**: Send floating emojis that appear on everyone's screen in real-time.

### 5. High-Fidelity Visualizers
Bring your music to life visually with canvas-based animations.
- Choose from 5 styles: **Bars, Waveform, Particles, Circular, and Pixel**.
- Access visualizer settings directly from the Player menu.

## 🎨 Personalization

### Dynamic Themes
Vibe On features a **Dynamic Theme Engine**. The app's primary accent color and background gradients automatically change to match the artwork of the song currently playing.

### OLED Mode
For devices with OLED screens, enable **OLED Mode** in Settings to enjoy pure black backgrounds and save battery life.

### Language Support
Choose your preferred language for music discovery and interface from the Settings menu.

---

## 💾 Offline Support & Downloads
- **Favorites**: Mark songs as favorites to quickly access them in your Library.
- **Caching**: The app intelligently caches recently played songs for smoother playback on unstable connections.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I sync my library?**
As long as you are signed in, your library syncs automatically in the background. You can also trigger a manual sync from the Library page.

**Can I use the app offline?**
Yes, your library and previously cached songs are available offline. However, searching for new music and Group Sessions require an internet connection.
