# Privacy Policy for Vibe On 🛡️

**Effective Date:** March 30, 2025
**Last Updated:** March 30, 2025

**Vibe On** ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application and streaming services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Data**: When you log in via Google Authentication or account registration (powered by Supabase), we collect your email address, display name, profile avatar URL, and user identifier (`user_id`).
- **User Preferences & Library Content**: Saved favorites, custom playlists, listening history, and application settings (such as theme preferences, font selections, and equalizer profiles) are linked to your account ID for synchronization across devices.

### B. Automatically Collected Information
- **App Usage Data**: Transient playback state data (such as current track, queue history, and group session interactions) processed locally to support media streaming features.
- **Device & Local Storage Data**: The application utilizes IndexedDB (`vibeon_offline`) and Local Storage on your device to store downloaded audio tracks, artwork images, and cached settings for offline playback functionality.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected information strictly for legitimate operational purposes:
- To provide, operate, and maintain media playback, streaming, and audio visualizer features.
- To synchronize your music library, playlists, listening history, and application preferences across your logged-in devices.
- To manage real-time group listening sessions (via Supabase Presence and Broadcast services).
- To authenticate your account securely using Google OAuth credentials.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Streaming Providers

Vibe On integrates with reputable third-party APIs and services to deliver audio content and backend functionality:
- **Supabase**: Account authentication, cloud database storage, real-time collaboration, and edge functions.
- **JioSaavn API**: Music search, stream retrieval, recommendation algorithms, and album artwork metadata.
- **LibriVox & Open Library APIs**: Audiobook catalog discovery and metadata retrieval.
- **Radio-Browser API**: World radio station directory listings and live audio stream links.

*Note: Third-party API providers receive standard anonymous technical HTTP requests (such as user-agent and IP address) necessary to serve audio streams and metadata. Vibe On does not share your personal account identity or email with content providers.*

---

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We **do not** sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or usage history. We do not share data with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

Information is shared only under strict operational circumstances:
- **Service Providers**: Backend database infrastructure (Supabase) that processes encrypted user records on our behalf.
- **Legal Compliance**: If required by applicable laws, legal process, or governmental request.

---

## 5. Security & Data Retention

- **Encryption**: All communications between the Vibe On app, Supabase backend, and audio streaming endpoints are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS / TLS protocol.
- **Data Retention**: Account information, favorites, playlists, and listening history remain stored as long as your account exists. Local offline downloads remain on your physical device until explicitly cleared by you or upon app uninstallation.

---

## 6. Your Data Rights & Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:
- **Access & Export**: You can export your saved music library and playlists in standard JSON, CSV, TXT, or M3U formats directly within the app settings (`Settings > Import & Export`).
- **Data Deletion**: You can request complete deletion of your account, synced settings, and database records by contacting us at our support address listed below or disconnecting your OAuth authorization.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy

Vibe On is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us to have it removed.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be posted within the application or repository with an updated effective date.
