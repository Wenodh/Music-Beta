I’m thrilled to share a major milestone in my journey with **VibeOn**. What started as a vision for a seamless music player has evolved into a premium, world-class streaming experience. 🚀

Lately, I’ve been pushing the boundaries of what’s possible in web and mobile engineering. By collaborating with **Google Jules**, I’ve been able to iterate faster and implement sophisticated features that bring VibeOn closer to a native-app feel than ever before.

🌈 **High-Fidelity Visualizers**: Music isn't just for your ears; it’s for your eyes too. I’ve added multiple canvas-based visualizer modes, including **Particles**, **Circular**, and **Waveform**, which react in real-time to every beat with glowing, high-performance animations.

🎚️ **Pro 10-Band Equalizer**: For the audiophiles out there—I’ve built a professional-grade graphic EQ. Fine-tune your sound exactly how you like it, with real-time audio processing.

☁️ **Cloud Sync & Auth**: Your library, everywhere. Integrated with **Supabase**, VibeOn now supports full user authentication and seamless cloud syncing. Your favorites and playlists stay with you across any device.

This project has been a masterclass in combining **React 18**, **Framer Motion**, and the **Web Audio API** to create something truly special.
